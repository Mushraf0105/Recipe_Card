Royal Indian Cuisine Origin: Hyderabad, India

Royal Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

An iconic culinary masterpiece perfected in the royal kitchens of the Nizams. Aged long-grain basmati rice is layered over chicken marinated in spiced yogurt, ginger-garlic, and royal garam masala, then finished with crispy fried onions (birista), fresh mint, and saffron-infused milk—slow-cooked using the ancient sealed dum pukht technique.

🌾 Naturally Gluten-Free 💪 High Protein 🏺 Traditional Dum Pukht ✨ Halal Certified
⏱️ Prep Time 45 mins
🔥 Cook Time 45 mins
⏳ Total Time 90 mins
🍽️ Servings 4 Servings
⚡ Difficulty Intermediate
🥗 Calories 640 kcal
🌿

Ingredients

1. Meat Marination

  • 800g Chicken thigh or bone-in pieces, washed & patted dry
  • 1 cup Whisked plain Greek yogurt (thick curd)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh ginger-garlic paste (1:1 ratio)
  • 1.5 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder & 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp Royal Shahi Garam Masala (cardamom, mace, cinnamon, cloves)
  • 1 cup Golden crispy fried onions (Birista), crushed
  • 1/2 cup Fresh mint and coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1.5 tsp Sea salt & 1 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice

2. Fragrant Basmati Rice

  • 500g Aged long-grain Basmati rice (soaked for 40 mins)
  • 2.5 L Water for rolling boil
  • 4 whole Green cardamoms, 2 black cardamoms & 4 cloves
  • 2 leaves Bay leaves (Tej Patta) & 1 star anise
  • 2 tbsp Salt (water must taste pleasantly salty)

3. Dum Layering & Aromatics

  • 1/2 tsp Pure saffron strands soaked in 1/4 cup warm milk
  • 3 tbsp Pure cow ghee (clarified butter)
  • 1 tsp Rose water or Kewra water (Nizami royal essence)
  • 4 halves Hard-boiled eggs & roasted cashews for garnish
👨‍🍳

Preparation Steps

  1. Deep Marination of the Meat

    Step 1 • 45 mins prep + 2 hrs rest

    In a heavy-bottomed brass handi or deep pot, combine the chicken with whisked yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, royal shahi garam masala, crushed golden fried onions (birista), fresh mint, coriander, lemon juice, ghee, and salt. Massage spices thoroughly into the meat cuts. Cover and let marinate for at least 2 hours (or overnight in the refrigerator for maximum flavor penetration).

  2. Parboiling the Fragrant Basmati Rice (70% Rule)

    Step 2 • 7–8 mins

    Gently rinse the basmati rice until water runs clear, then soak in water for 40 minutes. In a large pot, bring 2.5 liters of water to a rolling boil with whole cardamoms, cloves, star anise, bay leaves, and salt. Add the soaked and drained rice. Cook on high heat for exactly 6 to 7 minutes until the rice grains are lengthened but still have a firm grain center (approx. 70% cooked). Immediately drain through a colander.

  3. Artisan Layering in the Handi

    Step 3 • 5 mins

    Spread the marinated chicken evenly across the bottom of the handi. Gently spoon half of the warm 70% parboiled rice over the meat. Scatter half of the saffron milk, remaining crispy fried onions, fresh mint, coriander, and roasted cashews. Layer the remaining rice over top. Drizzle melted cow ghee, remaining saffron milk, and aromatic kewra water in concentric circles.

  4. The Sacred Dum Hermetic Seal

    Step 4 • 3 mins

    Roll whole wheat flour dough (atta) into a long rope and press it around the rim of the handi. Press the lid firmly onto the dough to create an airtight seal (alternatively, seal tightly with heavy double-layer aluminum foil under the lid). This traps all steam inside, forcing meat juices to vaporize through each grain of rice.

  5. Two-Stage Dum Cooking on Tawa

    Step 5 • 35 mins

    Place the sealed handi directly on medium-high heat for 8 to 10 minutes to build internal steam pressure. Then place a heavy flat cast-iron tawa/griddle under the handi, lower the heat to low, and cook for 25 minutes in its trapped aromatic steam (dum pukht). Turn off heat; do not break the seal yet.

  6. Resting & Gentle Nizami Service

    Step 6 • 15 mins

    Allow the handi to rest undisturbed for 15 minutes off the flame so the delicate long grains settle and firm up. Break open the dough seal and inhale the burst of saffron and spice aromas! Using a flat saucer or wide skimmer, gently scoop from bottom to top, lifting succulent meat and rice together. Garnish with boiled egg halves, roasted cashews, and serve hot with Mirchi Ka Salan and cool Burani Raita.
